Good to Know

Visitor Information

Tourist Information Center

Available in main towns like Parapat and Balige — a good first stop for local maps and guide contacts.

Parking

Most viewpoints and villages have informal parking areas, often with a small attendant fee.

ATM

Found in larger towns such as Parapat and Balige — remote villages are often cash-only with no ATM nearby.

Hospital

Regional hospitals are located in Balige and other main towns; smaller clinics serve outlying villages.

Police

Local police posts are present in every main town along the lake's ring road.

Gas Station

Available along the main ring road; fill up before heading to more remote viewpoints or villages.

Public Restroom

Usually available at ferry terminals, major viewpoints, and larger villages — bringing tissue is wise.

Travel Smart

Safety Tips

Wear comfortable shoes.

Carry cash.

Respect local customs.

Weather changes quickly.

Book ferry tickets early.
